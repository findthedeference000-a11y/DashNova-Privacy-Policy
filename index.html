Privacy Policy — DashNova

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Welcome to DashNova! Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how our game handles information.

1. Information We Collect

DashNova does not require account creation or directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

2. Local Storage

DashNova uses browser LocalStorage to save coins, high scores, settings, purchased power-ups, and game progress on your device. This information is not sent to our own servers. Clearing browser data may delete saved progress.

3. Third-Party Advertising

DashNova uses Adsterra advertising services. Adsterra and its advertising partners may use cookies, IP addresses, device information, and similar technologies to deliver advertisements, measure performance, and prevent fraud.

We do not control how third-party advertising providers process information.

4. Cookies

DashNova does not intentionally use cookies to store personal information. Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies.

5. Data Security

Game progress is stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account system or personal-data database for DashNova.

6. Children's Privacy

DashNova does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party advertising providers may process technical information when displaying advertisements. Advertising must comply with applicable age-related laws and policies.

We may update this policy when necessary. Updates will be published on this page.
